# Sniptape logo — usage guide

## The idea

The kept sound, punched out of a rounded capsule, with the trimmed-off ends right beside it: what Sniptape does, in one shape.

## Files

| Use | File |
|---|---|
| Symbol, colour (master) | `sniptape-symbol.svg` |
| Symbol, small sizes (≤ 48 px) | `sniptape-symbol-small.svg` |
| Horizontal lockup, colour | `sniptape-horizontal.svg` |
| Stacked lockup, colour | `sniptape-stacked.svg` |
| Wordmark only | `sniptape-wordmark.svg` |
| One-colour black / white / pine | `icons/sniptape-symbol-*.svg`, `lockups/*-black|white|mono-1c7b5a.*` |

| Favicon and web icons | `icons/favicon.ico`, `favicon.svg`, `icon-192/512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `site.webmanifest`, `head-snippet.html` |
| Presentation | `presentation.html`, `slides/` |

## Colours

| Name | HEX | RGB | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pine | `#1C7B5A` | 28, 123, 90 | Symbol on light grounds; app-icon tile; one-colour logo |
| Light pine | `#5FC199` | 95, 193, 153 | Symbol on dark grounds |
| Ink | `#141D19` | 20, 29, 25 | Wordmark |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | Reversed logo on pine, ink or photos |

Pine on white is 5.2:1 and light pine on the dark app background is 8.7:1, so both pass the 3:1 minimum for graphics. CMYK and Pantone matches are not defined yet; have a printer proof them before any print run.

## Typography

The wordmark is Nunito Bold (SIL Open Font License), converted to outlines. Never retype it; use the files.

## Clear space

Keep space equal to **the capsule's width ÷ 2** (48 units on the 256 grid) free around the symbol or lockup. Nothing else may enter that area.

## Minimum sizes

- Symbol: 16 px. Below 48 px, use `sniptape-symbol-small.svg` (fewer, wider shapes).
- Horizontal lockup: 120 px wide on screen, 30 mm in print.
- Stacked lockup: 80 px wide on screen.

## Backgrounds

- Light grounds (white, the app's light background): colour version.
- Dark grounds (ink, the app's dark background): light pine symbol with a white wordmark, or the all-white version.
- Pine, or busy photos: the all-white version only.

## Don't

- Recolour the symbol in anything other than pine, light pine, black or white.
- Add shadows, gradients, outlines or effects.
- Stretch, rotate, or rearrange the bars and capsule.
- Fill the slots in the capsule; they are holes, not white shapes.
- Place the colour version on pine or mid-tone backgrounds.
- Retype the wordmark in another font.

## Notes
