What we heard

The brief

A free, local desktop app for parents: paste YouTube links, trim the start and end of each track, save MP3s onto a child's SD card. It must feel simple and trustworthy, crafted and precise, and work as a Windows/macOS app icon at 16–32 px.

simpletrustworthycraftedprecise

Success: avoids the play-button and music-note clichés, reads at 16 px, works in one colour, and connects to the app's trim gesture.

Concept A

Capsule

The kept sound, punched out of a rounded capsule, with the trimmed-off ends right beside it.

  • Tells the product story: keep the middle, cut the ends
  • A solid, rounded silhouette that holds up as an app icon
  • A dedicated small-size version keeps it clean at 16–32 px
